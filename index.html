Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 July 2026

1. Who we are

Cutz ("we", "us") operates a marketplace connecting customers with independent barbers. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the rights you have under UK/EU GDPR.

2. What we collect

Customers: name, email, password, phone number (optional, not verified), profile photo (optional), booking history. Barbers: name, email, password, phone number (required), business address, services and pricing, and subscription/billing status.

3. Why we collect it

To create and secure your account (barbers must verify their email address before accessing the app), to let barbers and customers book and manage appointments, to let each side contact the other about a booking, to show barber availability and walk-in status on the map, and to bill barbers for their monthly platform subscription.

4. Who we share it with

A barber you book with can see your name and phone number (if you've added one) so they can contact you about your appointment, and you can see your barber's phone number on your booking confirmation. We use third-party processors (account/email verification, payment processing for barber subscriptions) strictly to provide the service — they don't get to use your data for anything else.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings, which erases your profile, bookings and reviews from our systems.

6. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time by contacting us. You can also withdraw cookie/analytics consent at any time from Settings.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@cutz.app.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 4 July 2026

1. The service

Cutz is a free booking marketplace for customers. Customers never pay through the app — booking and using Cutz costs nothing. Barbers pay a monthly subscription to be listed on the platform.

2. Barber subscription

Barbers pay £12.99/month to be listed, billed via a secure web checkout (not an in-app purchase). Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from the barber's profile; access continues until the end of the paid period.

3. Bookings

Booking a barber sends a request that the barber can accept or decline. Customers may cancel an upcoming booking free of charge up until 24 hours before the appointment. Cancelling within 24 hours of the appointment isn't possible in-app.

4. Account standing

To keep the platform reliable for barbers, an account that cancels 5 bookings in a row will be automatically closed.

5. Reviews

Customers may leave one star rating (1–5, no written comment) per barber, per completed booking relationship. Barbers cannot respond to ratings.

6. Acceptable use

Don't use Cutz to harass barbers or customers, submit false bookings, or attempt to circumvent the subscription or review system.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms? Email support@cutz.app.
