About Cutz

Getting a haircut
shouldn't take three DMs.

Cutz is a booking app built around how people actually get their hair cut: find a barber nearby, see who's free, book or walk in, and get on with your day.

Barbers are some of the busiest people we know. Their booking tools weren't keeping up.

For most people, booking a cut still means messaging a barber on Instagram and waiting for a reply. Is he open today? Can he fit me in before six? By the time the answer arrives, the slot is gone.

Walk-ins aren't much better. You turn up, see four people waiting and have no idea whether it's twenty minutes or two hours.

Barbers pay for it too. Every hour answering messages is an hour not cutting. No-shows leave empty chairs. And the platforms that promise to help often take a commission on every client they send.

So we built Cutz. A live map of the barbers around you, real prices and availability up front, booking that confirms instantly, and a walk-in queue you can join from your phone.

Our mission

Keep every chair full,
and every client on time.

01

Free for customers. Always.

Booking a cut on Cutz costs nothing. No booking fees, no card on file. You pay your barber for the cut, like you always have.

02

Barbers keep what they earn.

One flat monthly price and zero commission. We make money when Cutz is useful to barbers, not by taking a slice of every cut.

03

Reliable on both sides.

A 24-hour cancellation window and no-show protection mean a booked slot actually means something, for the barber and the client.

04

Your data isn't the product.

We don't sell personal data or run third-party ads. Read exactly what we collect in our privacy policy.
