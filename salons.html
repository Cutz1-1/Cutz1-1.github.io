Salon FAQ

Good questions.

Who counts as a staff member?

Every active barber in your salon apart from you, the owner. The 300 kr base covers the salon and you, and each active staff member adds 50 kr a month.

What happens to the bill when someone joins or leaves?

Your price follows the size of your active team. Add a barber and it goes up by 50 kr a month; remove one and it goes down. You're billed monthly through Stripe.

Do you take commission from my barbers?

No. Clients pay your barbers directly, and Cutz never takes a percentage of any booking.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from the app. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Full details are in the Terms of Service.
