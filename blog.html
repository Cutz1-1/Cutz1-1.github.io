What's new in Cutz: features we've shipped, offers for barbers, and updates about the company.

Company29 September 2026

Cutz is on the App Store, with a new home at cutzapp.one

Download Cutz on iPhone, and meet the new website for customers, barbers and salons.

Cutz is now available to download on the App Store for iPhone. Find barbers near you, see who's free right now, and book ahead or join a walk-in queue.

We've also rebuilt cutzapp.one from scratch. It now has a dedicated page for barbers with pricing and the LAUNCH offer, plus an updated privacy policy and terms of service written around how Cutz actually works.

Questions or feedback? Email cutz.support@gmail.com. A real person reads every message.

Barbers28 September 2026

LAUNCH: 3 months free for the first 10 barbers

Enter LAUNCH when you sign up as a barber and your first three months cost nothing.

To welcome our first barbers, the code LAUNCH gives you 3 months of the Barber subscription for free.

It's limited to the first 10 barbers who redeem it, and each account can use it once. Nothing is charged during the free months. When they end, your subscription continues at 300 kr/month unless you cancel first.

The LAUNCH panel on our barbers page shows live whether the code is still available.

Company25 September 2026

Security hardening ahead of launch

We tightened access controls across the platform before opening the doors.

Before launch we reviewed how data moves through Cutz and closed several gaps, tightening who can read and change what at the database level.

Most of this is invisible, and that's the point. Your account, your bookings and your barber's business details are only reachable by the people who should see them. Read more in our privacy policy.

Barbers24 September 2026

Manual bookings for barbers and salons

Clients who phone or walk up to the counter can now go straight into your Cutz calendar.

Not every client books through the app. Barbers and salons can now add a booking manually for clients who call, message or ask in person.

Manual bookings use the same time slots and the same double-booking checks as app bookings, so your calendar stays the single source of truth.

Barbers24 September 2026

Referral free months, handled properly

Free months from a referral code now carry through to billing, and we remind you before they end.

When a code like LAUNCH gives you free months, those months are now applied as a trial period in billing, so there's no charge until they're over. The subscription screen shows your free months clearly instead of 'first month charged today'.

We also send a reminder push notification before your free period ends, so the first charge is never a surprise.

Product20 September 2026

No more double bookings

Two clients can no longer book the same barber at overlapping times.

Cutz now blocks overlapping bookings on the server. That covers identical times and also a longer service that would run into the next slot.

If someone grabs a time a moment before you, you'll be asked to pick another instead of both of you turning up for the same chair.

Product20 September 2026

Prices shown in Danish kroner everywhere

Barber profiles on the web now show prices in kr, matching the app.

Barber profile pages on the web used to guess a currency from your browser, so 200 kr could show up as £200. Prices now always appear in Danish kroner, exactly as they do in the app.

Barbers19 September 2026

Salons arrive on Cutz

Put your whole shop on one profile: team calendars, join codes, shifts and time-off requests.

Cutz now supports salons. A salon gets one profile on the map listing every barber, and clients choose who they want to book with.

Owners invite staff with a join code, set shift schedules, handle time-off requests and manage the shop from a salon dashboard. Solo barbers can upgrade their existing account to a salon and keep their bookings and ratings.

Salon pages also have shareable links at cutzapp.one/s/…, which open straight in the app on iPhone.

Product19 September 2026

Reschedule requests and a waitlist

Need to move a booking? Ask for a new time. Fully booked? Join the waitlist.

You can now request to reschedule a booking instead of cancelling and starting over.

If the day you want is full, join the waitlist for a time window. We'll send a push notification as soon as a cancellation opens a slot.

Product8 August 2026

Book from a link, right in your browser

Every barber has a shareable profile link, and clients can book on the web.

Barbers can share their Cutz profile link anywhere: Instagram bio, WhatsApp, a sign on the mirror. Anyone who opens it sees the barber's services and prices and can book online with a Cutz account.

On an iPhone with Cutz installed, the same link opens the profile directly in the app.
